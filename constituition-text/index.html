The whole text, as images and as a PDF

constituition.org · prototype, 11 Oct 2026 · real text, rendered by the site’s own Typst setup

The content comes first: no logo, no rules, no labels in capitals, no accent colour. Each image has the article and its title (on image 1), the text, and one small grey line with the link and “2 / 5”. Images after the first say where they start: “Article 19(3), continued”.

Each idea shows the same four articles: 21A (short), 19 (typical), 124A (struck down; the court’s note is on image 1) and 366 (the longest). Images show at about phone-feed size. Tap one to open it full size.

The A4 print copy with the same treatment: no logo or nameplate. Every page links to the article’s own page, and the link is clickable. The small line above the title gives the source and date. 124A now carries the court’s note, which the current PDF leaves out.
